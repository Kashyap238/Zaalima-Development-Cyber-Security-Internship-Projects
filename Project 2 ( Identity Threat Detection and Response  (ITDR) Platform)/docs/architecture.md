## Architecture

Identity Events
↓
Event Parser
↓
Threat Detection Engine
↓
Risk Scoring Engine
↓
Behavior Analytics
↓
Anomaly Detection
↓

- Detect Account Takeover
- Detect Identity Abuse
- Reduce Lateral Movement
- Prioritize High Risk Users
