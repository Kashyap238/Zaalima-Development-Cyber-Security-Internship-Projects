# Risk Scoring Model

The platform calculates risk scores based on detected threats.

Examples:

Impossible Travel = +20
MFA Fatigue = +30
Privilege Escalation = +50
Token Theft = +40

Risk Levels:

0-29 = Low Risk
30-59 = Medium Risk
60-79 = High Risk
80+ = Critical Risk

Purpose:

- Prioritize investigations
- Highlight critical accounts
- Improve SOC visibility
